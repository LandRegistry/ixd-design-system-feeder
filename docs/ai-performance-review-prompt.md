## What this does

Gives you a report as to how you are doing - in regard to your skills on the DDaT framework.  

Use for your performacnce reviews and converstaions with your line manager, and to help with documenting examples for DDaT Pay Framework evidence


To use the prompt 

run it as is, on any Ai tool that can access your work communications and documents.  Attach a copy of your objectives, and any other information you want it to use as evidence.  If it can't access the internet, attach a copy of the skills for your DDaT role. This has been used in Copilot.


```
//Context and role

I am preparing for my mid-year civil service review next week.

//Goal

Review and summarise my work achievements and feedback.

//Action

- summarise my key achievements from the past 6 months by reviewing my emails and messages. 
- summarise the work against my objectives (attached).
- also look for nuggets, less frequent, or one off things that might be very significant, but are not frequent.
- give me one or two really good specific examples for each summary point.

Also, highlight any positive feedback I have received from stakeholders and team members.

-----

how does my work measure against the DDAT Capabilty framework for my role: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/interaction-designer#lead-interaction-designer 


----------

Compare this against the DDAT capabilty framework for an Interaction designer specifically lead and head.


Assess how strong my evidence is for each skill. Write an example or two for each skill to use as evidence. The examples should be less that 400 words per skill



```

### Script history

This script originated from discussions about how to most effectively summarise mid-year, how a designer is doing - there are multiple things to review progress against, that overlap and at the end of the year will require a load of admin tasks - this it to help minimise the admin as well as help withj reflection and dicsussion on perfermance
