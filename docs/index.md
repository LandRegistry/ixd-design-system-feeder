## What is this site for - whats here?

Guidance drafted that is useful to share, that may develop into formal guidance in the HMLR design system.  

Make things open: it makes things better.  Share early, welcome feedback and collaborate.

The pages here will range from initial thoughts and drafts to pages almost 'ready to go'.

Information is created here (in the form we'd expect to pubish in - html/markdown) for sharing openly, to make re-use, and publication to a more formal place easier.

All information here should be taken as not yet proven, and reach out to the author and Interaction design lead (Andy) for advice before following.  (todo: add an easy way to contact us/authors)

Good practice for each page or topic:

- say how good (proven) the guidance is
- if Ai has been used - attribute it, and share the prompt too
- say who authored it, and the date and version


Please feel free to fork this repo use it for you're purposes,  improve it via a pull request, or reach out - to collaborate on anything here.
